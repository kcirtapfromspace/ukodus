# Survey: novel mathematical methods for solving Sudoku

Compiled 15 September 2026 from three parallel bounded web searches (optimization/algebra; logic/CP/SAT with certificate output; statistical physics and structured neural methods), excluding baselines already recorded in this directory (standard IP models, basic L1 recovery, dancing links, Berthier's CSP-Rules, classical consistency studies). Provenance: items marked with a primary link were retrieved by the searching agents; this file does not independently re-verify their quotes or claims. No one was contacted; nothing was posted.

**Headline for this workbench:** the one line that produces *small auditable arithmetic certificates for single deductions* — the direct methodological neighbor of this program — is the certified-solving/explainable-CP stack (§1). Its core paper was not in the priority audits and belongs on the attribution watch. The optimization and physics families (§2–3) mainly address whole-grid feasibility, with some rigorous guarantees whose assumptions and fractional/integral scope must be distinguished.

## 1. Certificate- and explanation-producing methods (closest to this program)

1. **Elffers–Gocht–McCreesh–Nordström, *Justifying All Differences Using Pseudo-Boolean Reasoning*, AAAI 2020** ([PDF](http://eprints.gla.ac.uk/204158/7/204158.pdf)). Every domain-consistent alldifferent propagation (matching/Hall-set eliminations) is certified by a polynomial-size cutting-planes derivation over pseudo-Boolean inequalities, machine-checked by VeriPB; a few add/divide lines per elimination. **Attribution-watch item:** these are multi-step derivations with division, so they sit on the sequence-complete side of the [five-source audit's](five-source-certificate-prior-art.md) key distinction (like Bezem–Nieuwenhuis) — PARTIAL relative to C1's one-equation unit-weight format, but the closest existing per-elimination arithmetic-certificate machinery.
2. **Bleukx–Flippo–Bogaerts–Demirović–Guns, *Using Certifying Constraint Solvers for Generating Step-wise Explanations*, AAAI 2026** ([arXiv:2511.10428](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10428)). Transforms Pumpkin DRCP proof logs into step-wise explanations, replacing repeated MUS extraction; proved step correctness. The Sudoku benchmark contains 100 expert QQWing puzzles made inconsistent by a nontrivial mistaken entry. [Full text, §§3.1, 5 and Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.10428v1).
3. **The KU Leuven explainable-CP lineage.** Bogaerts–Gamba–Guns, AIJ 300 (2021) ([arXiv:2006.06343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06343)): an explanation step is a minimal unsatisfiable subset of facts+constraints entailing one literal; greedy min-cost sequences reach the propagation fixpoint (soundness and sequence-completeness proved; per-step optimality not). Gamba–Bogaerts–Guns, IJCAI 2021/JAIR ([arXiv:2303.11712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11712)): OCUS gives provably cost-optimal steps via implicit hitting sets + MIP. Extensions: sequence simplification (CP 2023), symmetry-aware MUS (AAAI 2025, [arXiv:2412.13606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13606)), preference-learned cost functions (AAAI 2026, [arXiv:2511.10436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10436)), search-tree explanations (CP 2026, [Dagstuhl](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CP.2026.62)), and the deployed *Sudoku Assistant* (AAAI/IAAI 2023).
4. **Auditable CP solvers** (Gocht–McCreesh–Nordström CP 2022; McIlree–McCreesh IJCAI 2024): full solvers whose every propagation is justified inside one VeriPB proof.
5. ASP justification systems (xclingo, [arXiv:2009.10242](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.10242)) are engineering-grade for Sudoku.

**Contrast with this workbench:** the MUS/OCUS notion optimizes step *cost* over an unrestricted step vocabulary and proves nothing about a fixed restricted grammar; this program fixes the grammar (one ±1 combination, interval-or-gcd terminal) and proves exact completeness limits (five/six). The two are complementary: their pipelines could adopt the five-source guarantee; this workbench could emit VeriPB-checkable proofs.

## 2. Optimization and algebraic methods (whole-grid; a few real theorems)

1. **Dukes–Nimegeers, *The linear system for Sudoku and a fractional completion threshold*** ([arXiv:2310.15279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15279), 2023–24): full eigendecomposition of the Sudoku inclusion system's normal matrix plus a perturbation argument proves every sufficiently large, sparse rectangular-box Sudoku admits a fractional completion. A rigorous fractional-feasibility result; it does not guarantee integral Sudoku recovery.
2. **Tovey–Liang** ([arXiv:2009.04018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.04018), 2020): Under convergence assumption A.4 and a nondegeneracy condition, Proposition 4.8 gives a local asymptotic error bound O((√5/5)^k) for the Sudoku product-space formulation, independent of grid size. This does not guarantee convergence on arbitrary inputs.
3. **Wang–Zhang–Yan–Cai** ([arXiv:1605.01031](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.01031), 2016): Numerical uniqueness tests classify a collection of 49,151 minimal 17-clue puzzles into unique and nonunique L1-minimizer groups. CVX and YALL1 fail on the nonunique group in that experiment; nonuniqueness does not imply every minimizer is wrong. With the reweighted-L1 warm-restart line ([arXiv:1507.05995](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.05995), empirical).
4. **SATNet** ([arXiv:1905.12149](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.12149), ICML 2019) with the mixing method ([arXiv:1706.00476](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00476)): low-rank SDP MAXSAT relaxation as a differentiable layer; solver convergence proved, Sudoku exactness empirical.
5. Quantum/Ising ([arXiv:2510.19835](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19835), QIC 2025; [SPE 2026](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/spe.70063)): heuristic, but usefully documents why Sudoku QUBOs are worst-case instances.
6. Decision diagrams: the bounded searches located no matching Sudoku-specific MDD theory; related results include decision-ZDNNF exact-cover counting ([arXiv:2604.14627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14627), 2026) and generic peel-and-bound ([arXiv:2205.05216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.05216)).
7. **Search finding:** no matching published Nullstellensatz/polynomial-calculus degree bounds for Sudoku encodings were located in these bounded searches; the NulLA machinery for graph coloring ([arXiv:1503.04680](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.04680)) transfers in principle.

## 3. Statistical physics and structured neural methods (end-to-end; no certificates)

1. **Transient chaos / ODE solvers**: Ercsey-Ravasz–Toroczkai ([arXiv:1208.0370](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.0370), 2012): exact map to a deterministic ODE whose escape rate defines a hardness measure tracking human ratings; continued by memcomputing (Chaos 2026) and oscillator networks ([arXiv:2508.02250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02250)).
2. **Freezing transition**: Prates–Lamb ([arXiv:1810.03742](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03742), 2018) — backbone fraction jumps sharply versus clue density; empirical scaling. Ancestor: Williams–Ackland Potts spin-glass ([arXiv:1212.1649](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.1649)).
3. **Belief propagation with proofs**: Atkins–Sayir ISIT 2014 ([arXiv:1407.4328](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.4328)) — density-evolution thresholds for Sudoku-code erasure decoding via permutation invariance. No matching plain-9×9 convergence theorem was located. A precise question must specify the factorization, updates, damping, initialization, and convergence target.
4. **Neural reasoning with Sudoku as canonical benchmark**: IRED energy-based diffusion ([arXiv:2406.11179](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11179), ICML 2024); HRM ([arXiv:2506.21734](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21734)) and TRM ([arXiv:2510.04871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04871), 87.4% on Sudoku-Extreme) recursive test-time-compute models — with the ARC-Prize audit and mechanistic critique ([arXiv:2601.10679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10679)) attributing gains to outer-loop refinement; masked-diffusion multi-granularity ([arXiv:2410.14157](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14157)); RRN ([arXiv:1711.08028](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08028)); constraint-loss recurrent transformers ([arXiv:2307.04895](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04895)); SATNet's leakage controversy ([arXiv:2312.11522](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11522)). All empirical; none emit deductions.
5. **Sinkhorn lineage**: Moon et al. 2009 (easy puzzles only); no matching Sudoku application of a neural Sinkhorn/Latin-square combination was located in these searches (Gumbel-Sinkhorn [arXiv:1802.08665](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.08665); LinSATNet [arXiv:2407.13917](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13917) reports no Sudoku).

## Opportunities this survey exposes for the workbench

1. **VeriPB as an interchange target.** Emitting the workbench's interval/gcd certificates as VeriPB-checkable cutting-planes lines would make them auditable by an independent ecosystem, and the five-source completeness theorem is exactly the kind of guarantee the explanation pipelines (§1.2–1.3) lack for restricted step vocabularies.
2. **Nullstellensatz degree bounds for Sudoku** (§2.7) are a potential certificate-complexity direction; historical absence remains unverified.
3. **Specify a BP experiment** (§3.3), then test whether known fractional five-cycle states produce incorrect or unstable fixed points. A structural connection remains conjectural.
4. The tridagon-in-certificate-grammar and seven-source determinant questions from [the theorem survey](recent-theorems-survey-2026-09-15.md) remain the nearest in-house method extensions.
